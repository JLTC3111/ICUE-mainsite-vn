Privacy Policy

Commitment to protecting your personal information and privacy

Overview

The Institute of Economic, Urban and Construction Research (ICUE) is committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data.
🔒 We strictly comply with Vietnam’s personal data protection regulations and the EU’s GDPR.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:
  • Personal information: Full name, email, phone number, address
  • Professional information: Work experience, education, skills
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device used
  • Interaction information: How you use the website, pages viewed, access time
  • Cookies and tracking data: To improve user experience
Data Type Purpose of Use Retention Period
Contact information Respond to requests, send project updates 3 years or until deletion is requested
CVs and application records Evaluate candidates, store application records 2 years after application
Website data Analysis, website improvement 12 months

How We Use Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:
  • Provide consulting and customer support services
  • Process applications and manage HR
  • Send information about projects, events, and cooperation opportunities
  • Improve website and service quality
  • Analyze trends and usage statistics
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

Information Security

We apply strict security measures:
  • SSL/TLS encryption for all transmitted data
  • Up-to-date firewalls and antivirus software
  • Strict access control with multi-factor authentication
  • Regular data backups and recovery plans
  • Staff training on information security
  • Periodic third-party security audits

Information Sharing

We may share your information in the following cases:
  • With partners: When necessary to execute a project or service
  • With law enforcement: When required by legitimate authority
  • With service providers: Third parties supporting website and service operations
  • In case of M&A: If ICUE is merged or acquired
⚠️ We DO NOT sell or rent your personal information to third parties for commercial purposes.

Your GDPR Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:
  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we store
  • Right to rectification: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of personal data (under certain conditions)
  • Right to restrict processing: Temporarily stop processing data in certain cases
  • Right to data portability: Receive data in a readable format
  • Right to object: Refuse processing of data for marketing purposes
To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze traffic and user behavior
  • Improve website performance and experience
  • Provide relevant content and advertising
You can manage cookies through your browser settings or see detailed cookies policy.
