GDPR Rights

Understand and exercise your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

What is GDPR?

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the European Union (EU) privacy and data protection law that came into effect on May 25, 2018.
🇪🇺 GDPR applies to all organizations that process personal data of EU citizens, regardless of where the organization is located.
At ICUE, we are fully committed to complying with GDPR regulations to protect your privacy and personal data, especially in our international cooperation with EU partners.

Your GDPR Rights

Under GDPR, you have 8 fundamental rights regarding your personal data:

1. Right to be Informed

You have the right to know:
  • What type of personal data is collected
  • The purposes of data use
  • How long the data is stored
  • Which third parties may access the data

2. Right of Access

You have the right to:
  • Request confirmation of data processing
  • Receive a copy of your personal data
  • Know how your data is being processed
  • Receive this information free of charge (unless excessive requests are made)

3. Right to Rectification

You have the right to:
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Provide additional information if incomplete
  • Update outdated information
  • Receive a response within 1 month

4. Right to Erasure / “Right to be Forgotten”

You may request data deletion when:
  • The data is no longer necessary for its original purpose
  • You withdraw consent
  • The data was unlawfully processed
  • It is required for legal compliance

5. Right to Restrict Processing

You may temporarily halt data processing when:
  • There is a dispute over data accuracy
  • The processing is unlawful but you don’t want deletion
  • The data is needed for legal purposes
  • A pending objection is being verified

6. Right to Data Portability

You have the right to:
  • Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Transfer it to another service provider
  • Request direct transfer (if technically feasible)
  • This applies to data processed automatically

7. Right to Object

You may object to:
  • Processing for direct marketing purposes
  • Processing based on legitimate interests
  • Processing for scientific research
  • Automated profiling

8. Rights Related to Automated Decision Making

You have the right to:
  • Not be subject to 100% automated decisions
  • Understand the logic behind automation
  • Request human intervention
  • Challenge automated decisions

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise your GDPR rights, you may contact us through the following steps:
1

Identify Your Request

Select the GDPR right you wish to exercise and prepare the necessary details.
2

Submit Your Request

Use the form below or email us at privacy@icue.vn with details.
3

Verify Identity

We may ask for ID verification to protect your data.
4

Processing

We will handle requests within 30 days (with a possible 60-day extension for complex cases).
5

Receive Response

You will receive detailed feedback on the outcome of your request.

Processing Time & Fees

⏰

Standard Timeframe

We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.
📅

Extensions if Needed

For complex cases, we may extend up to 60 days and notify you of the reason.
💰

Fees

Most GDPR requests are handled free of charge. Fees may apply for excessive or repetitive requests.
⚡ Urgent requests: If justified, we will prioritize faster processing.

Supervisory Authorities & Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your GDPR request, you have the right to:
  • Internal Complaint: Contact ICUE’s Data Protection Officer (DPO)
  • Regulatory Complaint: File a complaint with the relevant data protection authority
  • Legal Action: Take the matter to court if necessary
🏛️ For GDPR requests related to EU citizens, you may file a complaint with the national data protection authority in the relevant EU country.
