# Archived past-project photo credits — 30 October 2026

These standalone regional photo covers have been superseded by the user's project collages. The records below describe the individual photographs archived under `public/pastProjects/covers/originally_used/`; they do not identify the author or license of an entire project collage. The active covers are configured in `home-app/src/data/pastProjectCovers.js`. Existing project galleries are retained.

Archived photographs are WebP files resized without cropping or retouching to at most 2,000 px wide, with 480 px and 960 px responsive variants. Each archived derivative remains under its source license. These are regional illustrations rather than documentation of the completed ICUE projects.
